## Ruling: **ADOPT WITH CHANGES**

The design content is sound and faithfully carried: every pinned decision in the CR traces to the
adopted design (`docs/2026-08-19-subline-recon.md`) or the controller ruling
(`docs/2026-08-19-ruling-subline-recon.md`) with no invention, the two draft prose blocks in
empyrean are **byte-identical** to the CR's "verbatim" §3/§4 blocks (diffed mechanically), the
adoption condition contains no unexecutable clause of the CR-24 class, and the arithmetic checks
out everywhere I ran it. But the CR carries **one misattributed quotation that reaches the
normative §11.15 text itself**, one wrong line anchor for a quoted phrase, and one honesty flag
(the flipX tolerance) that the CR body states and the permanent log entry drops. Prior CRs were
3-for-3 on exactly this class; this one is not an exception, and two of its three instances are in
text that would live forever in the contract. Hence adopt-with-changes, not adopt.

## Judgment on the pinned design decisions — all APPROVED as drafted (subject to M1/M3 wording)

1. **The segmented-decode convention text.** Faithful to adopted option (ii), and correctly states
   only the *observable* convention (common to (ii) and (iii) for CRAM) rather than the mechanism —
   a server may implement either. The blanking-before/at-2560/after edge cases in the §6 text match
   the recon's clock model (`:200-207`) exactly, including the subtle "write in blanking before the
   active window recolours the whole row" case.
2. **The three pinned limits.** CRAM-only is the demand's own scope and traces to C-1..C-7 with the
   resolve-stage cost argument intact; instruction-granular is grounded in the by-value `now_mclk`
   architecture and honestly stated as a bound ("read it as bounded, not exact"), with
   F-SUBLINE-ACCESSMCLK registered; first-wholly-recoloured-row-still-N+1 is the right restatement
   and matters for exactly the reason given (three in-tree assertions restate the same way).
3. **The `pixel_attribution` sentence.** One sentence, as ruled (Q6); placement on the bullet that
   owns the disagreement claim is right, and the D14 symmetry argument is sound.
4. **The follow-up fold into §11.15.** All seven named items (ACCESSMCLK, DMASPREAD, HGRID,
   VCOUNT-PHASE, CRAMDOT-half-closed, INDEX/SH-repriced) match their recon registrations, figures
   included (~33 mclk, `$C00008` blast-radius reasoning).
5. **Supersession handling.** Correct amendment-log practice: `112d683` had no §11 entry to
   preserve, so there is nothing to rewrite in the log; the §6 block is replaced in place with an
   explicit supersession marker; §11.15's narrative records what the clarification was, why it was
   right when merged, and why it is superseded — the same "logs are records, not live text"
   discipline §11.14 applied to §11.3's echo. The sequencing (prose merges with the oracle-next
   merge, so `main` is never wrong in either direction) is the ruling's own, correctly carried into
   the draft's commit message as a do-not-merge-early guard.
