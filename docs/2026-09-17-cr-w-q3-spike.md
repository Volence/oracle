# CR-W Q3 spike: can each dock tab's painted text be attributed to that tab?

**Branch** `parcel/crw-spike`, base `ef2b06d`. **Toolkit** `egui-0.36.1`, `epaint-0.36.1`, `egui_dock-0.21.1`
(`~/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/`). **Code** `crates/oracle-player/src/crw_q3_spike.rs`
(test-only module), plus a `#[cfg(test)]` hook in `TabViewer::ui` (`crates/oracle-player/src/ui.rs:256-285`) and the
module line in `main.rs:72-73`. Nothing is wired into `emulator/screen_text`, the aether engine, the schema or the
contract. Headless only: no emulator MCP tool was called and no window was opened. What the owner SEES compared with
the harvest is Q2 and is not claimed here.

## 1. Verdict

**PASS in form 1.** Form 2 passes everywhere except one reachable arrangement, where it fails exclusivity.

Form 1 is the in-pass paint-list span: `TabViewer::ui` notes `ui.layer_id()` and `PaintList::next_idx()` around
the body's `match`, and `ctx.graphics(|g| g.get(layer))` is read after `build_ui` returns, before the pass ends.
It attributed every drawn tab's text completely and exclusively in every arrangement measured. That includes a tab
floated into an `egui_dock` window, open popups and tooltips, text boxes, a discarded first pass, and 1.25 and 2.0
device pixels per point.

Form 2 is `Plugin::output_hook` on `FullOutput`, attributing each text shape by clip-rectangle containment in the
body's recorded clip. It matches form 1 in every arrangement **except when a tab is floated into a window over
another tab's body**. There it attributes the window's whole body, and the window's tab title, to the tab
underneath. That arrangement is reachable in the shipped window, so form 2 is not a safe fallback.

## 2. Method and the ground-truth independence argument

**Driven code.** All of it is shipped code:
- `Loop::new` on the fixture ROM, with a listing that names an aeon-shaped object pool. Without one, Objects draws
  one refusal paragraph.
- `arm_for_measurement`: 16 breakpoints, a RAM watch and the profiler.
- 8 real `Loop::iterate` presents, so the panels have rows.
- Then `Loop::build_ui` for every measured present: the top bar, nav, transport, the real `DockArea` with
  `theme::dock_style`, and the eleven real bodies.

**Recording.** `probe::enter` and `probe::leave` run at the top and bottom of the production `TabViewer::ui`,
behind `#[cfg(test)]`. The probe is thread-local, and when none is installed the hook does nothing.

**Ground truth for tab T** is a multiset difference of two whole `FullOutput`s:

`truth(T) = text(FullOutput with ONLY T's body run) - text(FullOutput with NO body run)`

- Each arm runs in a fresh `egui::Context` on the same loop, dock, size, input, scale and 12 warm-up presents.
- A body is suppressed by returning before its `match`. `egui_dock` still draws the leaf, tab strip, frame and
  scroll area.
- A text key is the galley's source string, its origin and its clip rectangle, quantised to 1/100 point.
- **Why it is independent of both harvests:** the truth never touches a layer id, a paint-list index or a recorded
  clip rectangle. It reads the flattened post-`end_pass` output of runs in which the harvest's premise (which shapes
  lie between two indices) plays no part. What the two instruments share is egui and the bodies, which is what is
  under test.
- **Controls asserted before any verdict** (`check_controls`):
  - **Determinism:** two independent full runs paint an identical text multiset.
  - **Additivity:** full minus no-body equals the sum of every `truth(T)`, so no body's text depends on another
    body having run.
  - **Single pass:** the measured present ran one pass.
  - **Non-empty:** at least one body was recorded.
- The main gate also asserts:
  - the recorded drawn set equals every non-collapsed leaf's active tab, read off the `DockState`;
  - every body leaves in the layer it entered;
  - the span read after `build_ui` equals the span read inside the body's own call;
  - spans are disjoint and in draw order;
  - **the layer's paint list is empty once `run_ui` returns** (`after_pass_len == Some(0)`), so form 1 must read in
    the pass;
  - every tab has text in its focus arrangement (anti-vacuity).

**One transient was measured and settled.** With 4 warm-ups, focus Watchpoints painted its body clip at y max 991.2
with every body running and 992.5 with only its own. A scroll bar was still animating in `egui_dock`'s outer
`ScrollArea`. At 8 warm-ups the two were 989.5 and 989.6; at 12, both 989.5. Additivity went red at 4, which is how
it was found. This is a property of comparing two runs, not of the harvest.

## 3. Exclusions, measured

- **Combo box list:** `an_open_combo_box_list_lands_in_its_own_layer_and_is_not_attributed`. The Watchpoints
  address-space combo is clicked open.
  - Its four items land in `LayerId { Foreground, ...with(IdSalt::new("watch-space")).with("popup") }`.
  - Their clip is the full window width (`[0, 130] - [1600, 231]`).
  - In both forms, what the harvest missed is exactly those four runs, and nothing is foreign.
- **Tooltip:** `a_tooltip_lands_in_its_own_layer_and_is_not_attributed`. The pointer rests on the `write` checkbox,
  with `tooltip_delay` set to 0.
  - The hover text lands in a `Tooltip`-order layer.
  - Both forms miss exactly it and take nothing foreign.
- **TextEdit contents and hint text:** `text_edit_contents_and_hint_text_are_attributed_to_their_tab`. A typed
  `w_target` and the empty `stopAfter` box's hint `∞` are both in the Watchpoints span, and both forms pass. The
  target box's own hint is absent once it holds text (a control that typing took effect).
- **A `ScrollArea` inside a body** (Memory, Objects, Breakpoints, Watchpoints, Profiler, Effects) changes neither the
  layer (asserted: layer at leave equals layer at enter) nor contiguity (the verdicts).
- **`egui_dock`'s outer `ScrollArea`** (`leaf.rs:1390`) wraps the body call. Its scroll bars and the `Frame`
  background are painted after `TabViewer::ui` returns, or into a slot reserved before it, so they fall outside the
  span. They are not text. What it does do is animate its scroll bar over the first presents, which moves the body
  clip (the transient above).
- **Painter clips narrower than the pane:** `the_screen_overlay_in_a_pane_narrower_than_the_picture_is_attributed_in_both_forms`.
  - The Screen tab's armed notice is painted through `painter().with_clip_rect(picture)` in a pane narrower than
    the picture.
  - `Painter::with_clip_rect` intersects with the painter's own clip (`egui-0.36.1/src/painter.rs:73`), so its clip
    stays inside the body clip. Both forms pass.
  - Control: the notice's clip is cut exactly at the body clip's right edge.
  - A body that called `Ui::set_clip_rect` or `Painter::set_clip_rect` (which do not intersect) could break form 2.
    No production body in `ui.rs` does today.
- **Floating window:** see §1. The window's body is drawn in `LayerId { Middle, "window SurfaceIndex(1)" }`.
  - Form 1 records that layer per span and stays exact.
  - Form 2 has only rectangles, and fails.
  - Reachable: `DockArea::draggable_tabs(true)` (`main.rs`), `TabViewer::allowed_in_windows` defaults to `true`
    (`egui_dock-0.21.1/src/widgets/tab_viewer.rs:93`) and `Panels` does not override it, and `layout.rs:262`
    stores window surfaces.
- **Multi-pass:** `a_discarded_first_pass_is_harvested_from_the_pass_that_is_kept`. A fresh context's first present
  is two passes; the measured pass counts of 20 presents were `[2, 1, 1, ...]` for default, every-tab and focus
  Watchpoints (a scratch run of `present` at `e3d040b`, not committed). `Context::run_ui` re-runs the whole
  closure. With spans reset at the start of each pass, the in-pass read equals form 2's text from the kept
  `FullOutput`.

## 5. What the CR gets wrong or leaves out, for the implementing parcel

1. **§2.3 and §11 Q3 name form 2 as the fallback "if the in-pass read proves awkward". It is not a safe fallback.**
   It fails exclusivity whenever a tab is floated over another tab's body (§1), and a floating window is reachable.
   Form 2 is valid only while no window surface is drawn over a main-surface body. Form 1 has no such limit.
2. **§10's sketch says to push `(Tab, layer, start, end)` into "a per-present list the loop owns".** It must be
   **per pass**.
   - `Context::run_ui` re-runs the closure on a discard, and a fresh context's first present is two passes.
   - A list that is not reset keeps spans whose indices point into a drained list (M6).
   - The screen-text push sits inside `Loop::iterate`, which runs per pass, so the last pass's push wins; reset the
     list where `build_ui` starts.
3. **§10 and §2.3 read "the layer's paint list" as though every body shares one layer.** A floating window's body
   draws into a `Middle` layer of its own (§3). The reader must use each span's recorded layer, never
   `LayerId::background()` (M3).
4. **§5.2 and W1 list the undrawn cases as "a tab behind another in its pane, a collapsed pane".** They should also
   name floating windows, which are drawn bodies in the drawn set. The surface order "in the order the window drew
   the panels" is main surface first, then windows. In the default dock it is Screen, Registers, Pacing,
   Breakpoints, not `Tab::ALL` order.
5. **Empty text galleys exist.** TextEdits paint `Shape::Text` with an empty source (seen in M5's output: `""` runs
   at the add boxes). The CR's "runs with at least one glyph on the glass" already excludes them. W3/W4 fixtures
   must not count them as runs.
6. **W6/W8 and any clip-based conformance row must warm up.** A body's clip moves for several presents while
   `egui_dock`'s outer scroll bar animates. 4 warm-ups were not enough and 12 were (§2).
7. **W10 says "measured under `--bench` with `--dock every-tab`".** This spike measured a test harness around
   `build_ui`, not `--bench`, and without the `unrenderable` test. W10 still needs its own run. The figure above
   bounds the text walk only.
8. **`Context::graphics` is documented as "read-only access" but takes the context's write lock**
   (`context.rs:1044-1046`, `self.write(...)`). A harvest must not call it from inside another context closure
   (deadlock). The CR cites the line correctly; the lock is not mentioned.
9. **Citations re-verified and correct:**
   - egui: `context.rs:1044` (`graphics`), `layers.rs:109` / `:121` / `:186` / `:204`, `plugin.rs:44`,
     `widgets/label.rs:284`, `widgets/button.rs:373`;
   - epaint: `text_layout_types.rs:751` / `:951` / `:1012`;
   - egui_dock: `leaf.rs:1330`, `leaf.rs:1390`.
   - One addition: `Painter::with_clip_rect` intersects (`painter.rs:73`), which is why the Screen overlay does not
     escape its body.

## 6. What would make this verdict wrong

- **Headless, not the owner's window.** Paint lists are backend-independent, but eframe's real present was not run.
  If a real backend path adds per-layer transforms (`Context::set_transform_layer`; `to_global` is applied only at
  drain), in-pass coordinates would be layer-local while `FullOutput`'s are global. No player code sets one today.
- **Arrangements not measured:**
  - a tab being dragged (egui_dock's drag-and-drop preview);
  - a collapsed floating window;
  - several stacked floating windows;
  - native multi-viewport;
  - pointer-driven hover states other than one tooltip and one combo;
  - context menus.
- **Text-only instrument.** Boundary errors over non-text shapes are invisible (M1a). The question is text
  attribution, so this does not bear on the verdict, but it is not a proof about all shapes.
- **Fixture content.** The fixture ROM and a synthetic object listing, not a real aeon game. Attribution does not
  read content, but a body path that only a real game reaches (for example a body that opens its own `Area`, or calls
  `set_clip_rect`) is not exercised. Such a body would make form 1 incomplete (text in its own layer), not
  non-exclusive.
- **Determinism is assumed across contexts and was verified per arrangement.** An arrangement whose body text
  depends on wall-clock time between arms would need the same care as the Planes repaint counter, which is reset per
  arm (`settled_with`).
- **The hook position.** The recording is at the production `TabViewer::ui`, inside `egui_dock`'s `ScrollArea` and
  `Frame`. A shipped hook placed anywhere else (around `DockArea::show_inside`, or in a helper) is a different
  seam, and this spike says nothing about it.
