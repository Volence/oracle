## The three open questions, ruled

**8.1 — The bounded-list spelling: (γ), the flat spelling scoped to a row — ADOPTED, conditional on
M2.** The CR's §2.4 reading is correct and I verified it against the text, not the CR: clause (a) is a
MUST for policy-bounded lists (`protocol.md:497-505` @ `2735ac7`), and `callers` is policy-bounded by
construction, so (α) — the recon's plain-array-plus-boolean — is a carve-out from a normative MUST and
is **declined**: the two facts it drops (`total`, `returned`) are exactly the facts that make a top-N
list checkable against itself, and `callersTotal` is load-bearing here in a way `returned` usually is
not — it is the true edge count the reply-bound cap makes meaningful (§7.7). (β) — the full nested
container per row — is declined on the recon's own objection, which survives contact with the text:
three levels of `.items` and four scaffolding keys repeated per row, for lists that are already bounded
at `maxProfilerCallers`. (γ) satisfies clause (a)'s field set in full, at a scope §2.4 has not named:
its selection sentence (*"A list that is a **field** of a larger result is a container object … A list
that **is** the whole result keeps the flat spelling"*) sends every field-list to the container
spelling, so a §2.4 reader post-merge would conclude `callers` must nest, and would find §6 shipping
otherwise. The widening is right — but it must be **recorded in §2.4 itself**, not carried silently by
one blockquote (M2). The prefixing (`callersTotal` rather than a bare `total` as a row sibling) is
part of the row-scoped form and belongs in that sentence.

**8.2 — The `entryKind` enum: ruled AGAINST the drafted three-value spelling, FOR the split by cause —
`"hint" | "vint" | "root" | "depthCap"`** (M3). This overrules the consumer's literal proposal and
lands on the floor they named themselves — *"interrupt-entered edges keyed distinctly from the other
two, however spelled"* (anchor @ `6edb08ef`) — which the split satisfies twice over. The rejection is
argued openly, as the demand record is owed: **(i)** §6's own strongest profiler rule is that interrupt
accounting is keyed by **cause** and that `hint`/`vint` are never collapsed anywhere in the family —
the drafted `"interrupt"` value would be the single place in the profiler surface where the two causes
are indistinguishable, and the CR's §8.2 makes this argument against its own draft. **(ii)** The join:
the blockquote's own text says an interrupt-entered edge's caller *"is a bucket (this section keys
those by cause)"* — `hint`/`vint` join the edge to its bucket by name; `"interrupt"` cannot say which.
**(iii)** Widening an emitted enum later is **not additive**: a client or vendored validator closed
over three values refuses the fourth, so this is the one spelling decision that cannot be deferred
cheaply — the same shape-stability argument the CR itself makes about the biconditional. **(iv)** The
cause is free at the accountant: the recon records the caller frame kind as `Interrupt { level }`, so
the level is in hand at the push. The cost to the consumer is one two-value membership test in place of
one equality — and their stated branch (interrupt-vs-mainline) survives verbatim. Note in passing: the
CR's §8.2 calls this alternative *"a five-value split"* while listing four values — fix the arithmetic
with the spelling (folded into M3). The **sub-question** (a fourth-kind marker on a present
`callerAddr` for the inferred-root edge) is ruled as drafted: the biconditional **stands**, the fact
stays documented rather than typed, and `F-PROFILER-ROOT-EDGE-KIND` stays registered — nobody asked,
the mis-render risk is one edge per sample, and the CR's warning that widening the biconditional later
is a shape change is accepted as the price of not inventing unrequested surface now.

**8.3 — The edge's missing undivided partners: FOLD IN** (M4), the CR's own stated preference,
adopted. Add `cyclesTotal` and `cyclesSelfTotal` to the edge tuple, **required** on the edge as the
other undivided partner is. Grounds: the CR ships a **normative sum** — the `cyclesSelf` partition —
that no suite gate can assert with `==`; its own adoption clause 4 has to relax to a bound, which is
§11.16's *quiet gap* reproduced at the exact level the CR quotes the argument from, and `cyclesSelf`
is the field the consumer named as the money field. The demanded symmetry principle (*"an asymmetric
tuple invites exactly the mis-read your docs warn about elsewhere"*) argues for it; the consumer's
*"nothing missing"* was an answer to a question that did not put undivided cycle partners on the table
(the CR's §8.3 records this precisely), and the only explicit decline — per-edge stall — is untouched.
Required-on-the-edge is safe where required-on-the-reply was not: the edge shape has never shipped, so
§11.16's expired licence does not reach inside it. With the partners in, both edge-versus-row sums are
exact under `callersTruncated: false`, clause 4 gates with `==`, and `F-PROFILER-EDGE-UNDIVIDED`
dissolves into this adjudication's record instead of waiting for the first gate that cannot be
written.

**M2 — Record the flat-spelling widening in §2.4 itself.** §8.1 is ruled (γ), but §2.4's *"Two
