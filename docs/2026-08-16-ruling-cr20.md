# Ruling — CR-20, `emulator/read` (2026-08-16)

Un-framed adjudication of `docs/2026-08-16-cr20-unified-read.md`. Third of the session, after CR-18
(`2026-08-16-ruling-cr18.md`) and CR-19 (`2026-08-16-ruling-cr19.md`).

## Verdict: ADOPT WITH CHANGES — seven — and **build now**

"Right design, wrong time" was available and was explicitly rejected: the owner's MCP-first ruling was made
to gate exactly this item, the gate has been run, and its finding is the CR's evidence. Core needs nothing
new — `Vdp::cram`, `Vdp::vsram` and `System::z80_ram` are already public.

## ★ The factual error, which is the point of the pass

**The CR narrowed a catalogued bound. Twice.** `emulator/z80_read` is `addr` (0–`$3FFF`), `len` ≤ `$2000`
in §6, and the working legacy implementation agrees (`$0000-$1FFF` mirrored at `$2000-$3FFF`, max 8192).
The CR proposed `$1FFF` and "`len` ≤ 4096 as today" — which is not true of `z80_read` today at all.

Verified firsthand: `protocol.md:827` carries the catalogued bounds; `CRAM_SIZE = 0x80` and
`VSRAM_SIZE = 0x50` in `state_hash.rs`; `Vdp::cram`/`Vdp::vsram` at `vdp.rs:269/274`; `System::z80_ram` at
`system.rs:814`.

Third CR in a row, third defect of the same family: CR-18 quoted a sentence that does not exist, CR-19
named a symbol that is not the entry point, CR-20 tightened a bound against its own catalog.

## ★ Two "independent" questions that were one

The CR asked separately whether `read_memory`/`read_vram` survive, and what the collapse costs the MCP.
**Deprecate-and-keep answers both**: the MCP goes on calling both by name, so the measured churn is not
"one special case per tool" — it is **nothing**. The CR presented them as independent and they are not.

The §11.11 comparison also had to be *tested* rather than asserted: `press` survived `play_input` because
union-vs-replace is a genuine semantic difference, while `read{space:"vram"}` and `read_vram` are
identical. That makes this a real two-spellings case — and yet removal is still barred, by D5 and by a live
client. The catalog already had the mechanism: `emulator/wait_for_break` *(deprecated by `stopped`)*.

## ★ `z80` is not a space, and the reuse argument decides it against itself

The CR's whole case for the `space` param is that it reuses `watchpoint_add`'s enum **verbatim**. That
stops being true the moment the read enum holds a value the watch enum refuses — which manufactures
precisely the two-vocabularies-one-name drift the reuse was meant to prevent. Extending the *watch*
surface to the Z80 is unfunded work no client asked for; `z80_registers` and `z80_write` stay Z80-named
regardless; and the bounds mismatch above is the design saying the same thing a third way.

`z80_read` stays its own row. Implementing it is conformance, not design.

## ★ The argument the CR underplayed, and it is the best one

**A `cram` or `vsram` watch hit reports `space` and `addr` — and no method on this bus accepts that pair
back.** A client is handed a coordinate it cannot use. The unified read is not primarily a deduplication;
it is the **read half of a surface already shipped**. That belongs in the amendment ahead of any counting
argument.

## Corrections to the classification

"Three of the six return interpretations, not bytes" is overstated: **all three carry the raw content
alongside the decode** (`read_vsram.raw[]`, `read_vdp_registers.raw[]`, `read_cram`'s raw words). The
accurate ground is that they are **not address-shaped** — no `addr`, no `len`, results keyed by line, plane
or register name. Could they be re-expressed as unified read plus client-side decode? Mechanically yes; but
that deletes catalogued rows a live client drives, to save nothing, and discards the decode half that took
the work.

The count nit lands too: under deprecate-and-keep it is **2 built methods absorbed plus 2 never-built rows**,
not "3 → 1" — the CR's own arithmetic habit applied to its own arithmetic.

## What was missing, now pinned

Pure-read status (no `-32005`); `-32602` for a `symbol` with a non-bus space and for an unknown `space`;
`-32004` for a base out of its space and for a range whose end runs past, **refused never clipped**;
`len` default 1; `region`/`symbol`/`symbolDisp` present **iff** `space: "bus"`, schema-enforced in both
directions per the `old`/`fc` precedent; `caveat` declared but emitted **conditionally**, because
`read_memory`'s constant debug-read caveat is the anti-pattern §2.4's advisory names; and the adoption
condition — a conformant reply passing **closed** for each of the four spaces plus one refusal per bound.

## The lesson

Each of the three rulings this session found a different defect in the same place — the CR's evidence, not
its design. What survived every time was the *conclusion*. **The scope-correction habit is the CR's most
valuable contribution and its most reliable source of error**: three consecutive deflated headlines, and
three consecutive supporting-detail mistakes made while deflating them.
